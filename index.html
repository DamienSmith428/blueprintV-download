How it works

01

Zip your resource

Compress your entire FiveM resource folder (the one containing fxmanifest.lua) into a .zip file. Include your stream/ folder with the .yft models.

02

Upload & configure

Drop the zip onto the tool above, pick your template resolution and number of livery slots, then click Generate.

03

Download outputs

blueprintV scans all .yft models in your resource, generates a template PNG per vehicle, and produces ready-to-use carcols.meta and carvariations.meta files. Download the zip and drop the files into your resource.

04

Paint your livery

Open the template PNG in Photoshop or your paint tool of choice. The UV shells show exactly where each part of the vehicle lives on the texture. Name your textures {model}_livery_0, _livery_1, etc.

Desktop app

blueprintV for Windows

The full desktop app includes real UV shell extraction — reads your .yft files using CodeWalker.Core and renders every UV triangle as a wire-frame overlay on the template PNG. Supports drag-and-drop, carcols conflict resolution dialogs, and backup options.

Requires Windows · .NET Framework 4.8 (included in Windows 10 1903+ and Windows 11)
